About the Event

Programming Workshop 2026 is a one-day event organised by the Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics. Students can improve their programming skills and learn new technologies.

Speaker 1: Dr. Syad

Dr. Syad will introduce programming fundamentals and explain the importance of programming skills.

Activity: Coding Challenge

Students will participate in a coding challenge to test their programming knowledge and skills.
