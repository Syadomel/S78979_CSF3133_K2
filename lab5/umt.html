About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in 1979, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct practical sessions as well as research.

Eventually, the centre was upgraded to a branch campus in June 1996 and renamed Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). On 5 May 2001, the Cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) as an independent university college before it was officially renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007.

Today, UMT is widely recognized as Malaysia's premier marine-focused university, specializing in marine science, aquatic resources, maritime management, and environmental sustainability while offering a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
